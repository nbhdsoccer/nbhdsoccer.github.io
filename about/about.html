Mission & Vision

Mission

"To cultivate inclusive communities through elevated and welcoming soccer experiences—one person and one event at a time."

Vision

"To build meaningful connections that give our communities a sense of belonging while using soccer as a catalyst for positive neighborhood change."

Our Journey

2012

The Beginning

Four friends—Andre, Diego, Julian, and Jordan—started organizing pickup games in their neighborhoods. We are a Latino-led organization founded by neighbors who grew up on Chicago's South and West sides.

2015-2020

Growing Through Word of Mouth

Games grew organically. Neighbors brought neighbors. We learned consistency and genuine welcome create lasting bonds.

2021

Recognizing Our Impact

With 7,000+ community members, we realized we weren't just organizing games—we were building community infrastructure.

2022

Incorporating as Nonprofit

Incorporated as an Illinois nonprofit corporation, bringing organizational structure while keeping grassroots values.

2023-2024

Expanding Partnerships

Partnering with CPS schools, running memorial tournaments, supporting local artists, and expanding accessible programming.

2025

Achieving 501(c)(3) Status

Received federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status, enabling tax-deductible donations and expanded community development work.

"Great programming exists in South & West side neighborhoods—it's just hard to find." We bridge the gap by enhancing what already exists.
"What started as friends organizing pickup games has become something none of us expected. We're now partnering with CPS schools, raising funds for community causes, and running youth programs—but it all traces back to those early games where we learned that celebrating differences makes communities stronger."
