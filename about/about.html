Our story

Started by neighbors

In 2012, four friends, Andre, Diego, Julian and Jordan, started organizing pickup games in their neighborhoods. Neighbors brought neighbors, and the games kept growing.

We are Latino-led, founded by people who grew up on Chicago's South and West Sides. In 2022 we incorporated as an Illinois nonprofit. In 2025 we received federal 501(c)(3) status, so donations are tax-deductible.

Good programs already exist in South and West Side neighborhoods. They're just hard to find. We try to connect people to them and build on what's there.

Mission

Why we do this

Mission

To cultivate inclusive communities through elevated and welcoming soccer experiences, one person and one event at a time.

Vision

To build meaningful connections that give our communities a sense of belonging while using soccer as a catalyst for positive neighborhood change.
