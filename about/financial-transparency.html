Notes

What the numbers mean

Revenue

  • Pickup game fees are $5 to $10 payments from players at our weekly games.
  • Tournament registration fees are entry fees for organized tournaments, including the David Benavidez Memorial Tournament.
  • Community event collections are cash collected at tournaments and special events.
  • Sponsorships include support from Chicago Fire FC.

Expenses

  • Most of our spending is field and gym rental at the YMCA and Chicago Parks.
  • Equipment keeps games safe and open to everyone, whatever they can afford.
  • Event costs cover things like the annual Benavidez Memorial Tournament.
  • All but $40 in bank and payment fees went straight to programs.

Good to know

  • We had no paid staff in 2024. No revenue went to personal pay.
  • We received 501(c)(3) status in 2025. Donations are tax-deductible.
  • Our gross receipts are under $50,000, so we file Form 990-N (e-Postcard) with the IRS each year. You can confirm our tax-exempt status and filings with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search (search EIN 88-3862761).
  • We are working with the YMCA and community partners on summer leagues for South and West Side kids, and on after-school soccer with Chicago Public Schools.
