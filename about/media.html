Story ideas

What we can talk about

Pickup to nonprofit

Four friends started pickup games in 2012. Now: 7,000+ members, CPS partnerships, and $25K+ raised for community causes.

Youth soccer in CPS schools

REC 77 brings free soccer programs to underserved CPS schools. The YMCA partnership adds more kids.

A memorial tournament

The annual David Benavidez Memorial Tournament honors a co-founder and raises money for local health causes.

Open to everyone

Women's programming, games for every skill level, and spaces that welcome immigrants, across neighborhoods.
