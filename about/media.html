Quick Facts

  • Organization: Chicago Neighborhood Soccer (NBHD Soccer)
  • Status: 501(c)(3) nonprofit since 2025
  • Founded: 2012 as pickup games, incorporated 2022
  • Community: 7,000+ members across Chicago
  • Mission: Building community through accessible soccer
  • Leadership: 100% volunteer-powered, no paid staff

Story Angles

Grassroots to Nonprofit

Four friends started pickup games in 2012. Today: 7,000+ members, CPS partnerships, and $25K+ raised for community causes.

Youth Development

REC 77 partnership brings free soccer programming to underserved CPS schools. YMCA collaboration expands reach.

Memorial Tournament

Annual David Benavidez Memorial Tournament honors community member while raising funds for health causes.

Inclusive Community

Women's programming, all-skill-level games, and immigrant-welcoming spaces build bridges across neighborhoods.
