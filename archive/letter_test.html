August 18, 2025
To: DICK'S Sporting Goods Foundation and LISC

On behalf of Chicago Neighborhood Soccer, I am proud to offer this letter of support for our partnership with the Rauner Family YMCA and their efforts to expand access to youth sports and development programs in our community. We are excited about the facility and sports programming improvements that will be made possible through funding from DICK'S Sporting Goods Foundation and LISC.

Chicago Neighborhood Soccer is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that has been building community through soccer programming on Chicago's South and West sides for over a decade. With more than 6,500 community members, we understand firsthand the transformative power of accessible, high-quality sports programming in underserved neighborhoods.

Together with the Rauner Family YMCA, we are committed to breaking down barriers to participation and creating inclusive, high-impact opportunities for young people to grow through sport. The Rauner Family YMCA has been a trusted and mission-driven partner, working alongside us to:

  • Provide safe, structured environments for youth to engage in physical activity
  • Promote teamwork, leadership, and personal growth through sports
  • Ensure equitable access to equipment, coaching, and facilities for all youth—regardless of background or income
  • Create community-centered programming that responds to local needs and builds neighborhood connections

Our partnership with the Rauner Family YMCA exemplifies our shared commitment to community development through sports. Through our annual David Benavidez Memorial Tournament, hosted at their facility, we have channeled thousands of dollars directly into local health initiatives while creating meaningful opportunities for healing and connection across neighborhoods.

Support from DICK'S Sporting Goods Foundation and LISC would be transformational in helping us deepen this work. It would allow us to reach more youth, enhance program quality, and strengthen the long-term sustainability of our shared efforts to use sports as a catalyst for positive community change.

We enthusiastically endorse the Rauner Family YMCA's application and look forward to continuing our partnership to advance youth sports and development in Chicago.

Sincerely,

Andre Daniel Paredes, PhD
Co-Founder, President
