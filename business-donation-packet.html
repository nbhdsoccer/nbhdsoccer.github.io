About Chicago Neighborhood Soccer

Chicago Neighborhood Soccer (NBHD Soccer) is a community-driven nonprofit organization focused on building stronger communities through soccer in Chicago's South & West Side neighborhoods. We operate with 100% volunteer leadership, ensuring every donated dollar goes directly to community programming.

📍 Community Impact

Direct programming in underserved Chicago neighborhoods with measurable community engagement

💯 100% Program Funding
