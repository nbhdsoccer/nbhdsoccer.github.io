Community games help fund youth soccer

Our adult games and community events bring people together. Our tournaments and fundraisers raise money for youth programs and local causes.

When you play, enter a tournament, or come to an event, you help pay for soccer for kids in our neighborhoods.

Getting started

How it works

Pick a game on Meetup. Show up. Meet some people. Come back next week. Nobody is going to quiz you on your skill level or ask how long you've played.

Between games, the community talks on Discord: game updates, coordination, and conversation.

Looking for more pickup in the city? OpenFutbol is our open source tool for finding pickup soccer across Chicago.

Help keep the game going

None of us get paid. Your gift goes to fields, equipment, insurance, and more youth programs in the neighborhoods that need them most.
