Weekly pickup

Show up and play

We've run pickup in Chicago since 2012. About 400 people play with us every week. You don't need a team or a membership.

When
5 to 6 days a week, mostly 8 to 10 PM
Where
Rauner Family YMCA, South and West Side fields, and the suburbs
Cost
$5 to $10 a game. Some Saturday games are free.
Who
Adults, all skill levels. Coed except Women's Wednesday.

How to join

Three steps

  1. Join the NBHD Soccer group on Meetup. It's free.
  2. RSVP to a game. The cost and location are on each event.
  3. Show up a few minutes early. Equipment is included.

New players are welcome at every game. We play both recreational and competitive games.

Neighborhoods

Players from all over the city

We focus on the South and West Sides, and players come from everywhere. That includes Pilsen, Bridgeport, Little Village, Humboldt Park, Logan Square, McKinley Park, Back of the Yards, Garfield Park, Bronzeville, Chinatown, Lawndale, Austin, Englewood, Washington Park and Hyde Park.

Don't see a game near you? Ask us. We may have one close by, or we can help you start one.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be good?

No. All skill levels are welcome, from beginners to competitive players.

How much does it cost?

$5 to $10 a game. Some Saturday games are free. The price is on each Meetup event.

What should I bring?

Water, cleats for indoor or turf, and shin guards. Other equipment is included.

Are games coed?

Yes, every game except Wednesday. Wednesday night is Women's Wednesday, for women only.

Can I start a game in my neighborhood?

Yes. Email nbhdsoccer@gmail.com. Every day on the schedule is run by volunteer hosts. See how to volunteer.
