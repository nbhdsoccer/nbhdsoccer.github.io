Volunteer

NBHD Soccer is 100% volunteer-run. Every game, league and event happens because people give their time.

Get in Touch

From player to leader

Most of our leaders started as players

  1. Play. Come to a pickup game and get to know people.
  2. Co-volunteer. Help an existing host run their day. Learn the ropes.
  3. Lead. Host your own weekly game day. The day is yours to run.

From there, some people coach kids, help with events, or build tools with us.

How it works

Just send an email

There's no application. Email us and Andre will walk you through the options. We'll talk about what you're interested in, how much time you have, and where you can help most.

Email nbhdsoccer@gmail.com

Can't give time? Give money.

None of us get paid. Your gift goes to fields, equipment, insurance, and more youth programs in the neighborhoods that need them most.
