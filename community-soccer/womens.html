Women's Wednesday

Women-only pickup soccer every Wednesday night at the Rauner Family YMCA. All skill levels.

RSVP on Meetup Get updates

What it is

A night that's just for women

Most pickup soccer in Chicago is mostly men. The games can be fast and rough, and it's easy to feel like you don't belong.

So every Wednesday is women only. It's run by women in our community, for women. If you've never played, come. If you played competitively, come. More than 200 women have played with us.

When
Every Wednesday, 8 to 10 PM, year-round
Where
Rauner Family YMCA, 2700 S Western Ave, Chicago
Cost
$10 a game. Equipment provided.
Bring
Water, cleats for indoor or turf, shin guards

How to join

Sign up on Meetup

  1. Join the NBHD Soccer group on Meetup.
  2. RSVP to the Women's Wednesday event for the week.
  3. Come to the Rauner Family YMCA a little before 8 PM.

Want to play on other nights too? Our coed pickup games run most days of the week.

How it started

Here to stay

When we launched Women's Wednesday, we put a couple thousand dollars of our own money into the first two months so women could try it. In February 2023 we wrote to the community: it worked, and women's pickup is here to stay.

In the 12 months before that letter, more than 1,000 new members joined us on Meetup. Almost 20% of them were women.

Women's Tournament

Our annual women's tournament

We held our first Women's Tournament in September 2025, built around the Women's Wednesday community. We plan to run it every year.

FAQ

Common questions

I've never played. Can I come?

Yes. All skill levels are welcome, from never played to competitive.

Is it really women only?

Yes. Every Wednesday is 100% women.

Does it run all year?

Yes. Every Wednesday, year-round. It's indoors, so weather doesn't stop it.

How much is it?

$10 a game. Equipment is provided. The price is on each Meetup event.

Can I help lead?

Yes. Everyone is invited to be a leader in our community. See how to volunteer or email nbhdsoccer@gmail.com.
