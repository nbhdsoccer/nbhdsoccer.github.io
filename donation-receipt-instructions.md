# Donation Receipt Template Instructions

## Overview
The `donation-receipt-template.html` file is a fillable form template for creating official tax-deductible donation receipts for business donors to Chicago Neighborhood Soccer.

## How to Use the Template

### Step 1: Open the Template
1. Open `donation-receipt-template.html` in any web browser
2. The template will display with blank fields to fill in

### Step 2: Fill in Donor Information
**Donor Information Section (left side):**
- Business/Organization name
- Contact person name
- Complete mailing address
- Phone number
- Email address

**Receipt Information Section (right side):**
- Receipt number (suggested format: NBHD-2024-001, NBHD-2024-002, etc.)
- Date of donation received
- Payment method (check, Zelle, cash, etc.)
- Check number (if applicable)

### Step 3: Complete Donation Details
- **Total Contribution Amount:** Enter the dollar amount in the large box
- **Description of Contribution:** Check the appropriate box:
  - ☐ Cash donation
  - ☐ Corporate sponsorship  
  - ☐ In-kind donation
- **Specified Purpose:** If donor specified how funds should be used (otherwise write "General fund")
- **In-Kind Donation Details:** Only fill if receiving goods/services instead of money

### Step 4: Signatures and Date
- **Authorized Representative:** Sign and date as the organization representative
- **Donor Signature:** Optional for donor records
- Fill in both date fields

### Step 5: Create the Final Receipt

**Option A: Print and Fill by Hand**
1. Print the template
2. Fill in all fields with black or blue ink
3. Sign in person
4. Scan or photocopy for your records
5. Mail/email to donor

**Option B: Fill Digitally (Recommended)**
1. Use a PDF editor or browser to fill fields
2. Save as PDF with filled information
3. Email to donor
4. Print a copy for your records

## Important Legal Requirements

### What Makes It Valid
- ✅ Organization name and 501(c)(3) status clearly displayed
- ✅ EIN number included (88-3862761)
- ✅ Donation amount clearly stated
- ✅ Date of contribution
- ✅ Statement that no goods/services were provided in exchange
- ✅ Authorized signature from organization

### Record Keeping
- **Keep copies** of all receipts for at least 3 years
- **Sequential numbering** helps track all donations
- **Digital backup** recommended for all receipts

## Sample Receipt Numbers
Use a consistent numbering system:
- `NBHD-2024-001` (first donation of 2024)
- `NBHD-2024-002` (second donation of 2024)
- `NBHD-2025-001` (first donation of 2025)

## When to Issue Receipts

### Always Issue For:
- Any cash donation of any amount
- Corporate sponsorships
- In-kind donations over $250
- When donor specifically requests receipt

### Timeline:
- **Best practice:** Within 48 hours of receiving donation
- **IRS requirement:** By January 31st of year following donation
- **Large donations:** Same day if over $1,000

## Donor Communication

### What to Include When Sending:
1. Completed receipt (PDF format)
2. Brief thank you message
3. Reminder to consult tax advisor
4. Your contact info for questions

### Sample Email Text:
```
Dear [Donor Name],

Thank you for your generous donation to Chicago Neighborhood Soccer! 

Attached is your official donation receipt for tax purposes. Please retain this for your records and consult your tax advisor regarding deductibility.

If you have any questions about your donation or need any adjustments to the receipt, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Thank you for supporting our mission!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Chicago Neighborhood Soccer
nbhdsoccer@gmail.com
```

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues:
- **Missing information:** Don't issue receipt until you have complete donor details
- **Wrong amount:** Issue corrected receipt with new number, void the old one
- **Lost receipt:** Can reissue with notation "DUPLICATE" if donor needs replacement

### For In-Kind Donations:
- Donor determines fair market value (not you)
- Get detailed description of goods/services
- For items over $5,000, donor needs qualified appraisal
- You acknowledge receipt but don't value the donation

## Best Practices

1. **Double-check math** and all details before signing
2. **Use consistent formatting** for professional appearance  
3. **Keep digital copies** backed up securely
4. **Track receipt numbers** to avoid duplicates
5. **Review annually** to ensure compliance with current IRS rules

## Questions?
If you're unsure about any aspect of donation receipts, consult with:
- Your organization's accountant
- IRS Publication 1771 (Charitable Contributions)
- Local nonprofit support organizations
