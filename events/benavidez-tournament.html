Who David was

David Benavidez

David grew up in Chicago in an immigrant family from Ecuador. He finished his degree at UIC while working as a research assistant in the surgery department, and he was applying to medical school.

He volunteered at local health clinics and cared about health care for neighborhoods that don't get enough of it. He also brought people together through pickup soccer.

In 2018, at 23, David died of a rare blood disease. This tournament is how we keep his name on the field.

The tournament

His family comes every year

The Benavidez family travels to Chicago for the tournament. At the end of the day they hand out the awards and share stories about David.

It started as a small gathering of soccer groups that used to play separately. It came before Chicago Neighborhood Soccer became a nonprofit in 2022, and it's one of the things that brought us together.
