Annual events

Dates change each year. We post them on Meetup first.

Every fall · Rauner Family YMCA

David Benavidez Memorial Tournament

Our flagship tournament. It honors David Benavidez, a community leader and soccer organizer who passed in 2018. His family travels to Chicago for it every year. 100% of proceeds support local health initiatives.

David's story and the tournament
Every June · Art & community

South & West Side Art Gala

An evening for local artists from Chicago's South and West sides. Proceeds support emerging artists and creative programs in our neighborhoods.

Summer

Summer events

Here's what summer 2026 looked like. Watch Meetup for next summer's dates.

2026 recap

World Cup watch parties

For the 2026 World Cup we watched the big matches together at Ambassador in Little Italy, from Mexico's opener and USA vs. Paraguay to the Final.

Summer · North Avenue Beach

Beach soccer

Community beach soccer at North Avenue Beach. Free to play, with a $2 to $5 fee to cover water and food. In 2026 it ran in two stretches, mid-May to mid-June and mid-July to mid-August, each ending with a social outing.

Summer · Every other Friday

Friday socials

Every other Friday after pickup during beach soccer season, we head to Ambassador in Little Italy at 10 PM.

For weekly pickup, Women's Wednesday and every upcoming event, check meetup.com/nbhdsoccer.

Where the money goes

Events pay for youth programs

After costs, the money we raise at events goes to our Chicago Public Schools partnerships through REC 77. We're putting it into the schools and neighborhoods with the fewest resources.

The Benavidez tournament is the exception. Its proceeds support local health initiatives, in David's name.
