The Journey: From Pickup to Purpose

How neighborhood pickup games became a community development nonprofit

2012

Just Neighbors Playing Soccer

Started as informal pickup games among friends in Chicago neighborhoods. Simple community building through shared love of soccer.

2018

Community Growth & Organization

Expanded to regular programming. Built Meetup community of 6,500+ members across Chicago's South and West sides.

2022

Nonprofit Incorporation & Youth Programming

Formalized as Illinois nonprofit corporation and launched REC 77 school partnerships. Started volunteering and partnering on events that benefit youth in our area, enhancing existing community programs.

2022+

Scaling Community Impact

Applied for 501(c)(3) status and began scaling operations. Expanding to 12+ CPS schools, launching art galas, and building sustainable community development infrastructure through enhanced partnerships.

From Neighbors to Nonprofit Leaders

See how grassroots community organizing became a formal nonprofit serving 6,500+ neighbors. Our Co-founder Diego recently shared the journey with community member Mo - from pickup games to transforming neighborhoods through soccer.
