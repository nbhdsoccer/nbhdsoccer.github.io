How It All Works Together

Three programs, one mission: community funding youth

Our Model: Community Funds Youth

Every pickup game, every tournament, every community event directly supports youth programming.

We run three programs—NBHD Connect, NBHD Youth, and NBHD Events. Revenue from our community soccer and special events funds youth programming at partner organizations like CPS schools, the YMCA, and Boys & Girls Club.

These organizations need resource injections. We bridge the gap by enhancing what already exists—bite-sized, practical, and scalable. The more you participate, the more we give back.
