How it works

Adults play. Kids play free.

Schools and neighborhoods don't have the money for rec sports anymore. That's where we come in.

1 · Play

The community plays

Thousands of adults play pickup with us and come to our tournaments and events.

2 · Raise

We raise the money

Game fees, events, donations and sponsors. None of us get paid, so it goes back into the game.

3 · Give back

Kids play free

Free rec sports in our communities and shared spaces. Through REC 77, public schools never pay. We find the funding.
