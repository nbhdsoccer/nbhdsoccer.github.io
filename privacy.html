Privacy Policy

Plain answers about what we collect on this site and what we do with it. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Chicago Neighborhood Soccer ("NBHD Soccer", "we") is a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 88-3862761). This policy covers nbhdsoccer.org.

The short version: we collect very little, we never sell or rent your information, and you can ask us to delete anything you've sent us.

What we collect

  • Site visits. We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It uses cookies and collects things like your browser, device, approximate location (city level) and the pages you view. It does not tell us who you are.
  • Tournament registration. If you register for the David Benavidez Memorial Tournament, we collect your team name, your name, email, phone number, team size and any message you write. We use it to confirm your spot and contact you about the tournament. The form sends it to us by email through EmailJS, and a copy is saved in your own browser.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we keep the email so we can reply.

Donations

Donations are processed by Zeffy. We never see or store your card or bank details. Zeffy shares your name, email and gift amount with us so we can thank you and keep records, and Zeffy emails your tax receipt. Zeffy's own privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Other sites we link to

We link to Meetup, Instagram, YouTube and Discord, and embed some YouTube videos. Those services have their own privacy policies. Signing up for games on Meetup is covered by Meetup's policy, not this one.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.
  • This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information online. Youth program sign-ups happen through schools and partners, not this site.

Your choices

  • Email nbhdsoccer@gmail.com to ask what we have about you, fix it, or delete it.
  • You can block cookies in your browser, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.

Questions

Email nbhdsoccer@gmail.com.
