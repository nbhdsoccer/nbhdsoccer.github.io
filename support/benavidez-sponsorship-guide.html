David's Legacy Lives On

David Benavidez embodied the spirit of service. As part of an immigrant family from Ecuador who made Chicago their home, David understood firsthand the challenges many in our city's neighborhoods face. Despite obstacles, he excelled academically at UIC while working as a research assistant and volunteering at local health clinics.

Six Years of Community Healing: For six consecutive years, the Benavidez family has traveled to Chicago to participate in this moving ceremony. What began as a small gathering has evolved into one of our most anticipated annual events, consistently reminding participants that we are stronger together than in our separate silos.

Organizational Impact & Youth Programming

From Tournament to Year-Round Programming

The Benavidez Memorial Tournament is more than an event—it's a fundraising cornerstone that enables our expanding youth programming initiatives:

  • YMCA Summer Leagues: Structured programming for underserved South & West Side communities
  • After-School CPS Programs: Soccer combined with academic support and mentorship
  • Boys & Girls Club Partnership: Quality youth development through soccer experiences
  • Equipment Access: Ensuring all participants have quality gear regardless of economic background
  • Facility Partnerships: Consistent, safe spaces for community programming

Previous Tournament Impact

Community Health: Supporting health initiatives serving immigrant families in our neighborhoods

Children with Rare Diseases: Funding research and support for families facing rare medical conditions

Youth Soccer Programs: Equipment and programming for underresourced neighborhoods

Healthcare Mentorship: Programs for aspiring healthcare professionals in underserved communities

Our Track Record:

Partnership Process

How to Become a Tournament Sponsor

  1. Initial Contact: Reach out via email or phone to discuss partnership level and opportunities
  2. Sponsorship Agreement: We'll send a simple agreement outlining benefits and recognition
  3. Payment/Donation: Cash sponsors can pay via check, Zelle, or online. In-kind donors coordinate delivery
  4. Logo/Materials: Send us your logo in high-resolution format for marketing materials
  5. Tournament Day: Enjoy maximum visibility and community appreciation!
  6. Follow-Up: Receive impact report and photos showing your investment's community benefit

Timeline: We recommend confirming sponsorships at least 4 weeks before the tournament to ensure maximum promotional benefit and logo inclusion on all materials.
