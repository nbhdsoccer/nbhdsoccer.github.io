Why partner

Tax-deductible

How it works

Simple on purpose

No long proposals. Here is what happens.

The steps

  1. Email us. We talk about what you want to support.
  2. We send a simple agreement listing your benefits.
  3. Pay by check, Zelle or online. In-kind donors set up delivery with us.
  4. Send us a high-resolution logo.
  5. Show up on game day.
  6. Get an impact report and photos afterward.

What every partner gets

  • An official donation receipt for your taxes
  • Your logo and thanks where people will see it
  • A report on where the money went
  • A real connection with South and West Side families

Want something different? Tell us. We'll make it work.
