Support NBHD Soccer

Every contribution goes directly back to the community.

We are a volunteer-powered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. That means no executive salaries, no overhead bloat. What comes in goes back out into programming, equipment, and the kids we're trying to reach.

Where your support goes.

65% -- Programming

Coaches, referees, equipment, and everything it takes to run a league.

20% -- Charitable Giving

A portion of everything we raise goes back to local health and community causes.

10% -- Operations

Insurance, legal, and the basics that keep us legitimate and accountable.

5% -- Growth

Expanding to new schools and neighborhoods.

Ways to help.

Donate directly.
Contributions go straight to REC 77 and NBHD Youth programming. Every dollar helps keep the cost barrier low for schools and families.

Support us on Ko-fi

Donate equipment.
Soccer balls, cones, jerseys, and cleats are always needed. Reach out and we'll figure out the logistics.

Volunteer your time.
We run on volunteer hours. If you want to help with events, coach youth programs, or contribute a professional skill, we want to hear from you.

Spread the word.
Share our story. Bring someone to a game. Connect us with a school or a company that cares about this work.

For companies and foundations.

If you are looking for a direct, tangible way to invest in Chicago's South and West Side communities, we are a straightforward partner. No complicated tiers, no naming rights packages. Just a conversation about what you care about and how we can make something work together.
