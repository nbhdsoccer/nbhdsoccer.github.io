Subject: NBHD Soccer Program Update - Growing Impact with Community Partnerships

Hello [Name],

Even with just $19,000 annually, NBHD Soccer continues to grow and partner with major community organizations including the YMCA, Boys and Girls Club, and Chicago Public Schools (CPS). Our mission remains clear: Pickup to Purpose - transforming casual community soccer into structured youth development and community building.

🎯 CURRENT PROGRAM EXPANSION:
• YMCA Partnership: Developing summer leagues specifically for underserved South & West Side communities
• Boys & Girls Club Collaboration: Structured youth programming with academic support integration  
• CPS After-School Programs: Expanding into Chicago Public Schools for comprehensive youth development
• Community Events: Our signature 7th Annual Benavidez Memorial Tournament bringing families together

🤝 NEW PARTNERSHIP OPPORTUNITIES:
We've evolved our sponsorship approach to be more flexible and responsive to community needs:

• Ad-Hoc Youth Support: Help local players who can't afford fees participate in programming
• Benavidez Memorial Tournament (7th Annual): Title sponsorship opportunities for our signature community event  
• YMCA Summer League Funding: Support comprehensive programming for South & West Side youth
• Equipment & Facility Access: Ensure quality, safe programming regardless of participant economic background

Partnership Levels:
• Community Supporter ($1,000): Support youth teams and Benavidez tournament programming
• Community Champion ($5,000): Title sponsor recognition plus comprehensive youth programming support
