Transparency

How we handle the money

  • No salaries. Nobody gets paid. Our board is unpaid.
  • Big decisions are shared. We talk them through with the community, and we share board decisions.
  • Regular updates. We share program numbers with partners and supporters.
  • Checked by partners. Our work runs through established partners like Chicago Public Schools and the YMCA.
