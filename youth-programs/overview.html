How it works

Community funds youth

We are not a vendor selling programs to schools. We are a community group. We run pickup games, tournaments and events across the city, and we put that money and energy into programs for kids.

The more people play with us, the more we can do for kids in our neighborhoods.

Where we work

We focus on the South and West Sides because that's where we're from. We are a Latino-led nonprofit and we've been organizing soccer here since 2012. We know the families, the schools and what it's really like on the ground.

We pick schools that already have a gym, a field or a playground. Games happen on campus, so families don't have to find a ride.
