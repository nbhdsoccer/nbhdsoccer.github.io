What We Provide

  • Professional Coaching: Trained staff and volunteers
  • Equipment: Balls, cones, pinnies, goals
  • Curriculum: Age-appropriate soccer programming
  • Administration: Registration, scheduling, communication
  • Insurance: Liability coverage for programs

What Partners Provide

  • Space: Field, gym, or outdoor area
  • Students/Youth: Recruitment and enrollment
  • Staff Liaison: Point of contact for coordination
  • Buy-in: Institutional support for the program

Partnership Types

Schools

After-school programming, PE enhancement

Community Orgs

Youth centers, churches, nonprofits

Corporate

Sponsorship, employee volunteering

Foundations

Grant funding, program expansion

The Process

  • 1. Initial Conversation: Understand your needs and community
  • 2. Site Visit: Assess space and resources
  • 3. Program Design: Co-create curriculum together
  • 4. Pilot Season: Launch with one season
  • 5. Evaluate & Expand: Measure outcomes, grow together
