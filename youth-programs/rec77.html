About REC 77: Chicago Neighborhood Soccer's flagship school partnership program brings professional soccer coaching and equipment to CPS elementary schools. Also known as REC77, this NBHD Soccer initiative serves Chicago's South Side and West Side communities through authentic partnerships with Chicago Public Schools. Our nonprofit organization (nbhdsoccer) specializes in youth development through sports programming built specifically for each school's unique needs.
