REC 77

An in-house soccer league for Chicago Public Schools. It runs at your school, after school, with no travel. Every kid plays.

Talk to us about your school Sponsor a school

Why REC 77

Kids want to play. The options are gone.

Most kids on Chicago's South and West Sides don't have affordable sports close to home. Pay-to-play club teams start around $3,500 per kid per season, before travel. Comparable in-school programs cost $10,000 to $35,000 for one season. Most CPS schools can't pay that.

When kids don't have something structured after school, schools feel it in attendance, behavior and engagement.

REC 77 brings a real league to the school itself. The name stands for Chicago's 77 neighborhoods. We're starting with the ones we call home.

How it works

What it looks like at your school

We build balanced teams from your students and run refereed games every week. The season ends with a championship.

Season
6 to 8 weeks
When
3:30 to 5:30pm, 1 or 2 days a week
Grades
5th and 6th, or 7th and 8th
Size
6 teams (30 to 40 kids) or 8 teams (40 to 50 kids)
Games
20 minutes, small-sided

Small-sided games mean more touches and more kids involved. Teams are balanced, so there are no blowouts.

Cost

Free for schools. We find the funding.

REC 77 is free for schools. Public schools don't have the budget for rec sports anymore, so we don't charge them. We find the funding.

That money comes from sponsors, donors, and our own community games and events. We're looking for schools to grow with, not a transaction.

Where it runs now. REC 77 is active in two CPS elementary schools. Our goal is 12 schools and beyond.

Who we are

Why NBHD Soccer

We're a Latino-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We've organized soccer on the South and West Sides since 2012, and more than 7,000 people play with us. We grew up here and we still live here.

We also run the youth summer soccer league at the Rauner Family YMCA: game operations, refereeing and logistics.
