YMCA Youth League

We run the free youth summer soccer league at the Rauner Family YMCA, with YMCA of Metro Chicago.

Read the YMCA story Donate

What we do

We run the games

The YMCA hosts the league. We handle the soccer: game operations, refereeing and logistics on the ground.

It's free for kids. The goal is a safe place for kids to play, grow and belong.
